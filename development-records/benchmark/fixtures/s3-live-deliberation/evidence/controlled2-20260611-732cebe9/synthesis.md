# Synthesize

## Consensus
- issue-001 (high): The window-closure decision compares timestamps from two independent, undeclared clocks: enqueue stamps windowStartedAt from the ambient Date.now() (notification-batcher.ts:20) while flush judges closure against a caller-supplied now (notification-batcher.ts:30-32), and nothing on the type surface, doc surface, or claim set requires the two values to share a time base. All three lenses (logic, coverage, semantics) independently converged on the same defect, severity high stands unanimously, and the required closure is to designate a single time authority for the window lifecycle in the current target.
  - root cause: Window-lifecycle time authority is split between an ambient Date.now() sample at enqueue (notification-batcher.ts:20) and a caller-injected now at flush (notification-batcher.ts:30-32), with no element of the bounded target - type surface, doc surface, or claim set - declaring or enforcing that both values come from the same clock; designating a single time authority for the window concept would dissolve all three findings.
  - materiality: The defect sits exactly on the declared review focus - time-source semantics - and breaks the declared review goals of correctness, verifiability, and regression_risk. The failure condition needs no exotic input: any caller that drives flush(now) or fills enqueuedAt from a clock other than the ambient Date.now() epoch-ms timeline (fake-timer tests, monotonic-clock origins, skewed or cached timestamps) is a permitted input under the bare number type. Under such inputs the closure predicate stops corresponding to "the window has closed": the window can close immediately, late, or never - digest delay, premature emission, or flush returning [] forever - and the violation is silent because no surface exposes the shared-clock requirement. Clock substitution in tests is blocked, directly degrading verifiability.
  - action: Designate a single time authority for the window lifecycle. Either unify on caller time (accept now at both entry points, or derive window start from item.enqueuedAt) or unify on internal time (drop the now parameter from flush, or inject one clock provider used by both enqueue and flush - the injectable-clock option preserves testability). The weakest acceptable closure is documentation: state on flush that now MUST be epoch ms from the same clock as Date.now(), and state on the interface that enqueuedAt shares that authoritative clock basis. Closure obligation is must_close_in_target with action candidate fix_now: this is a current blocker on the declared review focus, and the chosen clock authority also supplies the clock basis that issue-004's action depends on, so this decision should land first.
- issue-002 (high): The flush comment (notification-batcher.ts:37-38) - the only written late-arrival contract of the batcher - promises that notifications enqueued between the window-close check and the reset are carried into the next window, but the code cannot implement that contract: the declared interleaving interval is unreachable in single-threaded synchronous JS, and items arriving in the gap that does exist (after window close, before the flush poll) are emitted in the closing digest and destroyed by the unconditional wholesale clear. C7 and C8 are not jointly satisfiable for any item present at flush time. All three lenses accepted the root and the bidirectional action; deliberation adjudicated the 2-vs-1 severity split to high, with logic and coverage retaining a recorded medium position that blocks nothing.
  - root cause: The flush comment's carry-over guarantee for late-enqueued notifications (notification-batcher.ts:37-38) has no realizing mechanism anywhere in the bounded target: nothing snapshots or attributes window membership at the close instant, enqueue has no window-close-aware branch and records no per-item attribution, and flush drains and clears pending wholesale; implementing window-membership attribution or correcting the comment would resolve all three findings.
  - materiality: The issue strikes contract-vs-behavior consistency - a declared review goal alongside correctness, maintainability, and regression_risk - at its most load-bearing point: the comment is the sole written statement of the batcher's boundary semantics for late-arriving notifications, and it asserts the inverse of observable behavior. Maintainers reason from a loss-safety guarantee that was never built, and consumers mis-attribute digests to windows. Deliberation made the materiality frame explicit: the issue's own recorded failure_condition names any maintainer or consumer decision premised on the documented carry-over as an in-scope failure path, so reliance on the written contract is the supported path, not a mitigating contingency, and against the recorded affected_purpose the failure is total.
  - action: Reconcile contract and behavior in one direction, as accepted by all three lenses. Either implement carry-over via per-item window attribution (filter the digest by enqueue time against windowStartedAt + WINDOW_MS, retain post-window items in pending, re-seed windowStartedAt for the retained set) or rewrite the comment to actual behavior ("all notifications pending at flush time, including any enqueued after the window closed, are emitted in this digest; nothing carries over"). Closure class is needs_decision with obligation must_close_in_target and candidates fix_now or accept_risk: the direction must be chosen first. Ordering matters - the decision also sequences issue-004 via dep-001 (the implement branch needs a trusted batcher-stamped per-item timestamp, which enqueuedAt today is not) and touches the dep-002 mechanism shared with issue-003 (the unconditional wholesale clear), while the roots of those issues remain distinct.
  - unresolved disagreement: A recorded severity disagreement remains: logic and coverage retain a principled medium position on the reliance-mediation frame (harm requires a maintainer or consumer to act on the falsified comment, the present emit-all-then-clear behavior is internally coherent absent the comment, and the gap is closable by a one-line contract correction). Deliberation adjudicated severity to high - the recorded failure_condition makes reliance on the documented carry-over the supported path rather than a discount, and against the affected_purpose the failure is total - and the remaining medium position of logic and coverage does not block the root, the action, or the severity ruling.
- issue-003 (medium): flush returns the digest and erases all pending state in the same synchronous step (notification-batcher.ts:39-41), and no recovery concept for downstream delivery failure (ack/commit, requeue, at-least-once) exists anywhere on the type surface: the error-handling/delivery-guarantee subdomain standard for a notification batcher is entirely unexpressed. If delivery fails after flush returns, notifications are irreversibly lost. Severity medium stands as raised with consistent stances and no deliberation needed; the issue stands independent of issue-002 despite the shared wholesale-clear mechanism, and a direction decision is owed before the next stage.
  - root cause: No collect-vs-commit separation concept exists in the design: flush couples digest collection and state erasure into one synchronous step, so no recovery path for downstream delivery failure can exist on the type surface.
  - materiality: The absence weakens the declared review goals of correctness and regression_risk on caller-facing failure-mode coverage, which material_kind_obligations require for this kind of component. The failure condition is ordinary, not exotic: any downstream delivery failure occurring after flush has returned finds pending already cleared, and the impact is irreversible notification loss with no ack, requeue, or at-least-once concept available to callers. Semantics independently confirms the contract-side half: the at-most-once loss semantics the code actually has is real but undeclared, hence invisible to callers. Medium severity is consistent with an undeclared-but-real loss semantics that leaves the happy path intact in the current target.
  - action: Choose one of two directions before the next stage (must_close_before_next_stage; candidates fix_before_release or accept_risk): split flush into two phases - return the digest, then erase state on commit/ack after successful delivery - or declare at-most-once semantics in the contract to place the absence explicitly out of scope. Semantics endorses the declare-at-most-once branch as the minimal semantic remedy; the collect-vs-commit split is a structural decision. Logic's advisory consistency obligation attaches to the split branch: if flush is split into collect and commit phases, the C5 empty-array return protocol and the window-restart rules must be redefined for the new uncommitted state, otherwise the fix introduces a fresh contradiction adjacent to finding-l3's sentinel overload. The dep-002 shared mechanism means the issue-002 direction decision touches the same wholesale clear, so the two decisions should be made with awareness of each other while remaining separate issues.
